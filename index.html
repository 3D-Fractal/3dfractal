What we're building.

3DF is building a portfolio of digital and niche business concepts. Some are active, some are being explored, and some are strategic investments.

Active

One-sliders.com

A global, multilingual platform for one-slider summaries of events, countries, sports, products and other high-intent topics.

The concept is built around simple, useful and commercially relevant pages that help users understand a topic quickly and continue toward relevant products, services or experiences.

One-sliders is designed as a scalable digital asset with global reach, affiliate potential and long-term content value.

A niche concept for custom-built audio experiences based on how people listen, where they listen and what matters most to them.

Exploring tailored speakers and sound solutions for homes, cabins, outdoor areas and personal listening environments.
